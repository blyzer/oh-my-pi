# Prioritized architectural backlog

> Part of the [OMP2 gateway-capabilities research](README.md). Deliverable 15. Audited commit `85d219ae7f`. Research proposal: nothing here is implemented.

The backlog is derived mechanically from the capability cards in deliverables 5, 5b, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 and 11 so that it cannot drift from them: every row is one card, with the card's own decision class. The tier is a rule, not a score: **P0** is a hand-picked set (see its rule below), the other tiers follow the card's decision class. Rows keep the order of their area document. Nothing is estimated in days; complexity is the card's own low/medium/high with its reason.

Total: 103 cards. P0 10, P1 26, M 17, P3 8, D 12, K 12, X 18.
