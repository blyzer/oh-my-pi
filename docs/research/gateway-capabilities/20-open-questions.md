# Open questions requiring architectural approval

> Part of the [OMP2 gateway-capabilities research](README.md). Deliverable 20. Audited commit `85d219ae7f`. Research proposal: nothing here is implemented.

Each question needs an owner decision because it touches a locked rule, an ADR, a credential or security boundary, a product intent, or a threshold this research declined to invent. Answers are not assumed anywhere in the set. Where a question has a recommendation, it is in the linked card.
