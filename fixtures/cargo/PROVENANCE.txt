cargo --message-format=json fixtures. Each <case>.stdout and <case>.stderr is the verbatim stream cargo wrote, never hand-edited.

Recorded on aarch64-apple-darwin with the stable toolchain: cargo 1.99.0 (5f94df478 2026-08-27), rustc 1.99.0 (b940084d7 2026-09-28), clippy 0.1.99 (b940084d7e 2026-09-28).
Each case ran in its crate's directory under /private/tmp/cargo-json-repro (neutral: no user name, no hostname) with target/ removed first, and stdout and stderr redirected to separate files, never through a pipe.

The commands are the shipped checkers' own arguments (crates/driver/src/cleanse/checkers.rs):
	cargo-check    cargo check --message-format=json
	cargo-clippy   cargo clippy --message-format=json -- -Dwarnings
	cargo-test     cargo test --message-format=json
plus one form no shipped checker runs, the human short format a discovered checker may declare with the `rust` parser:
	cargo-check-short   cargo check --message-format=short
